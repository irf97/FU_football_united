Football United Engineering Briefing
The product is the wedge. The network is the company.

A deliberately ordinary app,
hiding a server-less
proximity network.

Football United is matchmaking for pickup football. It is also the best distribution channel I can think of for a peer-to-peer, proximity-trust protocol — because the protocol's hardest requirement is football's normal behaviour.

Audience  Full-stack engineer Status  App live · protocol simulated to 10k TL;DR  Football gets the phones in one place
01 — The product

Find a real game, with real people, on a real pitch.

Ranked-queue mechanics for amateur/pickup football: discover a match near you, fill exact positions (a keeper-shaped hole is not a midfielder-shaped hole), get auto-balanced teams, play rated, then settle it with multi-party post-match peer voting. "Competitive queue + lobby," but the players show up in person.

02 — The stack as built

Boring on purpose.

Phoenix 1.8 / LiveView Elixir · OTP PostgreSQL PubSub realtime Tailwind v4

A contexted monolith — Accounts, Queues, Matching, Lobby, Balance, Matches, Voting, Ranking, Friends, Groups — plus two supervised GenServers (T-3h queue resolver, rank decay).

The one decision that matters: everything is event-sourced. Rank is an append-only ledger where every change carries its own breakdown ("why did my rank move?" is always derivable). Disputes are amendments, never mutations. Match completion is multi-party — captain submits, peers attest.

The app is not the moat. A good engineer rebuilds it in a sprint. That's the point — it's the incubator, not the invention.

03 — The real idea

Kill the server.

The app bundle and its data distribute peer-to-peer, BitTorrent-style. State doesn't live in a cloud; it lives on players' phones and propagates by physical proximity.

  • BLE / Wi-Fi-Aware pings — cheap, always-on presence and gossip.
  • NFC tap — the high-trust channel: key exchange, co-signing a match result.
  • Trust gradient — stranger → acquaintance (repeated co-presence) → friend (persistent replication).
  • Group-witnessed rank — your number isn't self-asserted; it's what your teammates hold. Forging it needs the whole clique to collude.
04 — Proven, to scale

Not vibes — a deterministic simulation, to 10,000 users.

Storage stays O(your network), not O(history) — flat from 1k→10k while matches grew 10×. Witness delivery recovered from a collapsed 5.5% to ~100%. The steady-state exchange is NFC-sized; rare bulk relays want BLE. One honest open problem: the friendless newcomer has no witnesses yet — the acquaintance tier is the likely fix, not yet validated.

05 — Why football markets the network

Every mesh project dies on the same rock: no reason to be near other nodes.

Briar needed a crisis. FireChat needed a protest. Mesh networks are a graveyard of "great tech, no co-located users." Amateur football is the rare domain where the network's hardest requirement is the product's default state:

01

Recurring co-presence is the activity

16+ people, same pitch, same time, every week. A guaranteed high-density gossip event with built-in repeat encounters — exactly what makes the relay model converge.

02

The graphs are one graph

Social graph = football graph = replication topology. Teammates are your witnesses; the squad is the trust quorum. The domain is the network's consistency model.

03

The copy is true

"No servers. Works with zero signal on the pitch. Your stats live with your team and can't be faked unless your whole squad lies." A story, not a feature list.

04

The viral loop is physical

To play, you bring people to a place. Every match onboards and seeds new nodes by construction. Acquisition cost is "show up to football."

06 — The wedge logic

You market the app. You're actually distributing nodes.

BitTorrent rode media. WhatsApp rode SMS-replacement. The move is to pick the most universal recurring physical gathering — pickup sport — as the friendly, low-stakes vertical that proves and seeds a general proximity-trust protocol in the wild.

Once it works for "who won the 5-a-side and is their rank legit," the primitive — proximity-witnessed, server-less, tamper-evident state — generalises to anything where local trust beats a central authority. Migration is favourable: the live build's event-sourced ledger is the P2P substrate. You swap the transport, not the domain.

07 — Be skeptical (credibility)

What I'd tell you to doubt.

  • The 10k numbers are a deterministic simulation, not field data. Real BLE/NFC is messier — range, pairing UX, iOS background limits, NFC being a centimetre tap not a pipe.
  • Identity migration is the real work and isn't done — self-sovereign keypairs replace SMS-OTP; everything depends on it.
  • Friendless bootstrap is genuinely open. Not solved. Don't pitch it as solved.
  • "No servers" still needs minimal bootstrap/discovery infra realistically. It's edge-tolerant, not literally zero.

Football is how you get enough phones in the same place, often enough, for a server-less trust network to become real.
