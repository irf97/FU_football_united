1 · Scope & Definitions

TermMeaning
QueueA scheduled match slot at a field with a format & per-position capacity. State: open → locked → confirmed → completed/cancelled.
Soft joinMembership status:"queued", not committed ("interested").
Lock-inA commitment (locked_at set). Leaving afterwards is penalised (§5).
Main positionGK / DEF / MID / FWD — the only vocabulary the matchmaking engine uses.
Sub-positionA display variant of a main (e.g. CB, CDM, ST). Never reaches matchmaking.
Witness(Network) a friend who replicates and vouches for your rank.

5 · Lock-in & Bail Penalties

ClauseStatementForce

Status

Validated in deterministic simulation to 10k users. Not field-implemented. Normative for the intended design, not the shipped build.

ClauseStatementForce
14.1Distribute state peer-to-peer; a device persistently replicates its friend graph and transiently caches proximity peers on a TTL.Must

Strategy · Edge Cases & Constraints

Edge Cases & Constraints

The boundary conditions that decide whether the system is correct, and the constraints the team operates under. Each is explicitly handled and test-locked unless flagged.

ConcurrencyLost-response on a consequential action

A 5xx/timeout after lock-in or finalize does not mean failure — the action may have succeeded server-side. Consequential operations are idempotent; the client re-reads authoritative state rather than trusting the failed response.

IntegritySub-position leaking into matchmaking

A player's chosen variant (e.g. CB) must never alter how they are slotted. The quota/fill/balance/matching engine reads only GK/DEF/MID/FWD; a dedicated invariant test asserts a "CB" player is matched as DEF.

LifecycleSoft vs locked leave at the 24h boundary

Soft members leave free. Locked members at exactly the 24h-to-kickoff boundary receive the 1-week tier (within-24h is inclusive). Timer expiry force-locks remaining queued members before resolution, so "queued but un-locked at deadline" cannot dodge commitment.

TerminationTeam balancing for non-8v8 formats

An earlier infinite-loop in team balancing for 7v7 was fixed with a strict-progress guard plus a fuel cap; balancing now provably terminates for every supported format, asserted by tests.

NetworkFriendless bootstrap (open)

In the P2P model, 7–8% of simulated nodes have no friends and therefore no persistent witnesses — their rank is not recoverable. Candidate fix: promote sustained acquaintances to provisional witnesses. Unvalidated; tracked as backlog BL-4.
